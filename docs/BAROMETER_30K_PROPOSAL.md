# Proposed 30,000 ft barometer change (owner review)

Status: **PROPOSAL — BMP581 remains the locked D08 part. Do not change the KiCad schematic, BOM fitted line, footprint or pin map on the basis of this document.** Reviewed 2026-09-22. The owner requires valid barometric altitude/vario to 30,000 ft in an unpressurized but physically protected aircraft cabin. The sensor must see representative static pressure through a reviewed enclosure vent; a temperature/accuracy acceptance range is still open.

## Trigger and comparison

The [NWS/WMO standard-atmosphere example](https://www.weather.gov/media/epz/mesonet/CWOP-WMO8.pdf) gives **300.9 hPa** at 30,000 ft. The locked [Bosch BMP581 DS004-13](https://www.bosch-sensortec.com/media/boschsensortec/downloads/datasheets/bst-bmp581-ds004.pdf), §2/Table 1, specifies **300–1250 hPa**: only **0.9 hPa (0.09 kPa)** of nominal pressure margin remains in that one reference atmosphere. Weather and static-port errors can consume it. The standard outside-air temperature is about **-44.5 C** ([NWS table](https://www.weather.gov/index.php/ama/conversions)), below BMP581's -40 C operating minimum; the board's actual temperature inside the cabin/enclosure is not yet known. Neither part below is thereby qualified for an unheated -44.5 C board.

## Electrical, firmware and mechanical impact to close before acceptance

- **Electrical:** ST Rev 6 §§3, 6–7 must supply a pin-by-pin application for 3.3 V VDD/VDD_IO, bypass, I2C selection, SA0 strap, reset/default and off-state behavior. The ST data sheet gives seven-bit I2C address `0x5C` (SA0 low) or `0x5D` (high); neither appears in the current [shared-bus map](INTERFACE_GPIO_MAP.md), but touch-address convention and bus-capacitance/off-state reviews remain open. Do not reuse the BMP581 schematic carrier, 0x46 address, fast-ramp resistor option or net names without review.
- **Firmware/interface:** device ID, register map, pressure conversion, ODR/filter/FIFO/interrupt options, pressure/temperature calibration and out-of-range handling change. Existing BMP581 driver assumptions cannot be reused. Keep the 100 kHz D22 bus policy and quantify vario noise/group delay for the accepted operating profile.
- **PCB/mechanical:** both devices are nominally 2 x 2 mm with 10 terminals, **not established as pin- or land-compatible**. Create a fresh ST-package-derived symbol and footprint from Rev 6 §10, independently compare land/pad/pin-1, obtain assembler mask/paste approval, then reassess vent, under-body routing, thermal island, footprint courtyard, placement and enclosure static-port response. The previous BMP581 footprint review cannot certify the replacement.
- **Temperature:** a 260 hPa pressure floor alone does not qualify operation if the board falls below -40 C. Define a guaranteed board temperature inside the aircraft/enclosure at the 30,000 ft pressure reference, or add a reviewed thermal design; review all other fitted components and the cells against the same envelope.

## Decision and evidence gate

The owner must explicitly accept a replacement of locked D08. Before changing the baseline, a worker must complete the exact LPS22HBTR pin/application matrix, manufacturer package/land review, selected-source five-piece availability and PCBA eligibility, firmware interface/test plan, pressure/temperature acceptance range and independent sensor/vent review. If cost, stock or low-temperature constraints reject LPS22HBTR, return an equivalent full proposal; do not infer that 2 x 2 mm parts are drop-in substitutes. No claimed 30,000 ft barometric performance is valid before bench pressure/thermal tests and the later built-board validation.
