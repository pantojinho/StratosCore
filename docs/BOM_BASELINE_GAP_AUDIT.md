# Pre-Astra engineering BOM gap audit

Audit date: 2026-09-22  
Scope: `bom/preliminary_bom.csv`, `bom/SOURCING_EVIDENCE.md`, `docs/ASTRA_HANDOFF.md`, and the exact-part/footprint records they cite. This is an audit and closure plan only. It does not select parts, declare DNPs, approve footprints, or change the planning BOM.

## Finding

The current CSV is a functional planning list, not an Astra-ready engineering parts baseline. It has 58 rows and no quantity column. Seven rows have `MPN=TBD`; `CELL_PROTECTION` also contains only competing families rather than an exact orderable code. Thirty-seven rows currently have `JLCPCB/LCSC Part Number=TBD` (recounted by CSV parser and corrected in O27; an older audit-log snapshot said 36). Packages are explicitly TBD for `MCU_MAIN`, `ADSB_MCU`, and the seven MPN-TBD rows; `CELL_HOLDER` has only an approximate envelope and `CELL_PROTECTION` has family-level package alternatives. `PASSIVES_DEBUG` is explicitly incomplete.

Sourcing evidence identifies six dated snapshot groups and 19 authorized-channel-only groups, but does not establish current five-unit PCBA pricing or assembler acceptance. A `TBD` supplier part-number field is a sourcing lookup gap; it is not evidence that the MPN itself is unresolved. Conversely, an exact MPN does not close package, footprint, factory-process, or assembly-status review.

The baseline also lacks an explicit assembly disposition for all optional or conditional hardware. D11 is a microphone provision with optional synchronized audio; `MICROPHONE` and `AUDIO_LEVEL_TRANSLATOR` need an owner-approved FIT/DNP policy and quantity treatment, with O07's switched-domain conflict, acoustic land/port, and footprint gates resolved first. `LORA_SMA_PIGTAIL` and `ADSB_SMA_PIGTAIL` are optional enclosure alternatives and need explicit per-configuration quantities/DNP treatment. Any other deliberately unpopulated footprints need a recorded DNP reason and assembly/test impact; do not infer DNP from “optional,” “provision,” or a candidate status.

## Footprint and package review gaps

The 17 files under `hardware/footprints` are candidates; parse/export success is not approval. The footprint README identifies independent dimensional second passes for MAX-M10S-00B, ICM-42688-P, MMC5983MA, BMP581, W25Q128JVSIQ, TPD1E0B04DPYR, U.FL-R-SMT-1(60), and ABM8-272-T3. It records assembler/process gates for these candidates. DIG-04 added eight support footprints from the KiCad 10 library; their exact-MPN second pass is still open. Thus no listed candidate should be treated as generally released merely because a file exists.

The footprint README explicitly leaves `USB4105-GF-A`, `BM12B-GHS-TBT`, `SKSCLCE010`, `ST1633I`, and the `MMICT5838-00-012` acoustic land open. Additional exact-package/footprint work remains for the locked ESP32 module and RP2040, SX1262, display/FPC connector pair, display translators, display reset device, LoRa/ADS-B RF candidates and both antennas, microSD socket, ADS-B SAW/LNA/detector/comparator, USB signal ESD and microSD ESD, holder/protection/gauge/charger and the unenumerated passives/test pads. For each block, the baseline needs a specific footprint identity and provenance, exact-MPN-to-pad/package comparison, independent review, and any required assembler mask/paste/courtyard approval. Cable housings, antennas, cells, display modules, and enclosure parts need mechanical/source definitions and quantity/disposition even where they do not receive PCB footprints.

The 37 supplier-ID `TBD` rows are the exact CSV lines to reconcile against the register before the engineering baseline is frozen: `MCU_MAIN`, `DISPLAY_FPC`, `TOUCH_FPC`, `DISPLAY_LEVEL_TRANSLATOR`, `DISPLAY_RESET_TRANSLATOR`, `DISPLAY_BACKLIGHT_DRIVER`, `BUTTON_1`, `BUTTON_2`, `RADIO_LORA`, `LORA_RF_SUPPORT`, `LORA_UFL`, `LORA_ANTENNA`, `GNSS_UFL`, `ADSB_MCU`, `ADSB_BOOT_FLASH`, `ADSB_CLOCK`, `ADSB_ANTENNA`, `ADSB_UFL`, `MICROSD_SOCKET`, `MICROSD_MEDIA`, `MICROPHONE`, `AUDIO_LEVEL_TRANSLATOR`, `USB_C`, `USB_CC_CONTROLLER`, `USB_SIGNAL_ESD`, `MICROSD_SIGNAL_ESD`, `USB_VBUS_PROTECTION`, `CELL_PROTECTION`, `FUEL_GAUGE`, `MAIN_3V3_BUCK`, `QUIET_3V0_LDO`, `LOGIC_1V8_LDO`, `LOAD_SWITCHES`, `EXPANSION_CONNECTOR`, `EXPANSION_HOUSING`, `PASSIVES_DEBUG`, and `GPIO_EXPANDER`. The 19 channel-only groups in `SOURCING_EVIDENCE.md` may cover several rows or repeated instances; check every exact MPN and quantity, and do not treat a group-level channel pointer as a supplier SKU, live stock, quote, factory-library match, or order authorization. `N/A` is appropriate for manual/final-assembly parts but still requires an identified authorized/local procurement path and quantity.

## Quantity, fit/DNP, and costing gaps

There is no planned quantity field or normalized count in the 13-column CSV. Before Astra, each functional block needs an explicit planned quantity or range and configuration basis; after capture, the integrator reconciles those plans to annotated references and final captured quantities. Known multiplicities that must be carried into that reconciliation include two `CELL_PAIR` cells; two `SKSCLCE010` buttons; three `U.FL-R-SMT-1(60)` locations (`LORA_UFL`, `GNSS_UFL`, `ADSB_UFL`); two separate `TPD4E05U06DQARG4` arrays (`USB_SIGNAL_ESD`, `MICROSD_SIGNAL_ESD`); three `TPS22918DBVR` load switches per PWR-04; two `BLB01` and two `TA2003A` instances per ADS-B candidate topology; and two labeled display samples for the sample gate. These are stated in source notes/status and are planning counts, not a released order quantity. RF antenna and pigtail options, microSD media, battery holder/protection/gauge, and passives need their quantities/configurations resolved rather than guessed.

Cost closure must use a dated, named-channel comparison for the initial five-unit target and account for the owner's D24 scope: Chinese assembled PCB plus display/touch, excluding local cells, local enclosure, freight, and taxes. Current sourcing evidence has no complete five-unit quote or verified assembly allocation, and several expensive/risk items lack an LCSC/assembler match. Keep price and stock TBD until a dated quote or displayed listing is recorded; use the 30-day freshness policy in the sourcing register and recheck at purchase. Cost feasibility is an input to baseline review, not a reason to silently substitute a component.

## Closure sequence

1. **Disposition and system inputs — product owner/integrator.** Set the intended baseline configuration and FIT/DNP/optional rules, especially audio, both optional SMA pigtails, display samples versus installed modules, cards, and prototype quantity. Preserve locked requirements. Any proposed baseline change goes through the decision register and owner acceptance.
2. **Close exact MPN and topology — subsystem owners.** Resolve the seven TBD rows and the family-level `CELL_PROTECTION`; explicitly state packages for ESP32-S3 module and RP2040. Power work follows the qualified-review gate; ADS-B/LoRa antenna and support decisions follow their independent RF references and stackup; microSD follows the storage qualification; passives are derived from closed application circuits. Capture exact manufacturer documents and revisions, source URLs, dated review, package drawing and relevant sections.
3. **Build package/footprint evidence — CAD plus independent reviewers.** Assign or create a footprint from the exact ordering code's manufacturer drawing for every fitted PCB component. Complete the DIG-04 exact-MPN second pass, deferred connector/custom lands, all non-candidate packages, and documented assembler process approvals. Record passives and test pads from reviewed blocks/DFT. Keep non-PCB items in the parts baseline with mechanical definition instead of inventing footprints.
4. **Define planned quantities and factory disposition — BOM integrator with block owners.** Expand aggregate rows, count repeated instances, identify alternates as mutually exclusive configurations, and record planned quantity/range plus FIT/DNP and reason. Astra can then assign schematic references; after capture, reconcile annotated references and quantities back to this frozen plan.
5. **Close sourcing and cost evidence — sourcing/manufacturing owner.** Reconcile every exact MPN to authorized channel, exact supplier SKU when applicable, dated stock/price or explicit channel-only status, assembly type, factory library/machine/process acceptance, and five-unit quote/allocation. Record unknowns as TBD; the current supplier-ID count of 37 was verified and reconciled in O27.
6. **Freeze the engineering baseline — integrator and required reviewers.** Produce the reviewed parts baseline with exact fitted/DNP items, passive values/packages, planned quantities, footprint/provenance/review status and sourcing status. Verify that every BOM and handoff entry gate is backed by evidence; only then update the shared workboard/status/handoff and permit Astra entry. The final orderable BOM still follows schematic capture and independent review.

Dependencies: the sequencing intentionally puts product disposition before count freeze, exact parts before footprint approval, and circuit/DFT closure before passives and quantities. The 2S qualified safety review, display controlled drawings/samples, RF review, enclosure fit, assembler process approvals, and unresolved system-interface/OQ work remain upstream blockers where they affect part choice, connectivity, or routing. Astra must not be used to decide those items.
