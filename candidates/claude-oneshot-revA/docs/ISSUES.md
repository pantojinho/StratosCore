Review date: 2026-09-23. Scope: everything in `candidates/claude-oneshot-revA/`. Severity: **BLOCKER** (must close before any order or energizing), **MAJOR** (wrong or unverified item that changes copper/nets), **MINOR** (cleanup/verification).

This list is the most useful part of the package for the Astra comparison: an item here is either a defect found in the repository baseline, a choice this candidate had to make where the baseline is silent, or an open gate that no CAD pass can close.

## C. Layout-level limitations of this one-shot

- **Open connections (5 after the 2026-09-23 refinement, was 11; see `outputs/drc_summary.txt`)**: SX1262 `LORA_RFO`/`LORA_RFI_P` (RF blocker) and GND at U2 pads 3/8, U3 pad 8 and U24 pad 2, which need local re-layout. The SHT40 SDA, both 3V3_MAIN links, C57 and three GND fragments (U7 buck exposed pad, ICM pads 9-11, BMP581 SDO/C35) were connected. Nothing is hidden by exclusions.
- **LoRa RF block**: topology only. The TX match / harmonic filter / RX balun parts are placed as generic 0402 positions around the SX1262 with TBD values; the block must be re-placed and hand-routed from the native E449 layout on this stack before any value or performance statement.
- Placement follows the floorplan zones; ICs have fixed engineering positions, and small parts are placed by an anchor search (engineering anchor map for hot-loop/protection parts: eFuse/ESD/CC controller at USB-C, charger passives and CD FET at the BQ25887, backlight diode at the TPS61169, SD switch/ESD at the socket, ADS-B switch at the RP2040). Decoupling loops, the BQ25887/TPS62130A hot loops and the TPS61169 SW loop are **not** hand-optimised.
- Routing is Freerouting + an A* clean-up router. RF paths (GNSS, ADS-B chain, LoRa) and the USB pair have no dedicated GCPW/differential geometry; RF claims require hand re-routing on the solver-confirmed stack.
- The ESP32-S3 module's antenna rule area (x <= 6.3 mm) holds no copper. Correction 2026-09-23: the former DRC error was a 0.09 mm overlap between J5 and the module-**body** courtyard, not the antenna keepout; J5 moved +0.4 mm. The full 15 mm off-board clearance still depends on the enclosure.
- `In1.Cu` is a continuous GND plane; `In2.Cu` carries signals, a `3V3_MAIN` pour in the core/LoRa area and GND elsewhere; outer layers have GND pours with ~400 automatically placed stitching vias. Shield-can areas are not designed.
- The display is modelled as an envelope 5 mm above the PCB on a printed frame (assumption); FPC tail exits, lengths and fold radius are TBD from the controlled Orient drawing.
- Enclosure: two-part concept (front bezel 11.5 mm incl. display window, back shell 25.5 mm incl. 2S holder) = 37 mm total, matching the planning envelope; no tolerance stack, snap/screw design or battery door mechanism.
